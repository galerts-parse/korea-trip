Itinerary Overview

Route: Gyeongju → Busan → Seoul (ICML) → Incheon

Dates: July 3–14, 2025 (12 days)

Pacing: Relaxed, solo-traveler friendly. No intense hiking.

Key Features:

  • Start in Gyeongju (1 day) — UNESCO tombs & temples
  • Bus from Gyeongju to Busan (~2h) — seafood markets & coastal temples
  • Intercity bus from Busan to Seoul (Jul 5, 7 PM) — arrives Seoul Nambu Terminal ~11 PM; taxi or subway to COEX
  • 7 nights at Grand Intercontinental COEX (Jul 5–12) — right at the conference venue
  • 2 nights in Jongno/Anguk (Jul 12–14) — traditional cultural experience, walking distance to Changdeokgung, Gwangjang Market
  • Clean one-way flow: South → Central → West (Incheon)

Don't forget to book in advance: View full checklist → Airport bus to Gyeongju · Changdeokgung Secret Garden (6 days ahead) · Mipo→Cheongsapo boardwalk (free, no booking)

~380km (Airport Bus) ~2h45m transit
TimeDurationActivity
10:00—Flight lands at Incheon Airport (ICN). Clear immigration, collect bags.

AccommodationGyeongju Hanok Guesthouse (e.g., Gyeongju Ran Hanok or similar). Traditional hanok compound with ondol rooms, steps from Daereungwon.
Trip.com Booking
FoodLunch: Grab snacks at Incheon Airport T1 for the bus.
Dinner: Hwangnam-ppang (황남빵) at Choi Yeonghwa Ppang — the original red bean bun shop.

Practical Tips

  • Incheon Airport → Gyeongju by bus: After immigration, follow signs to 'Intercity Bus' at T1. Free shuttle to Airport Intercity Bus Terminal (between T1/T2). Buy ticket for '경주' at counter or machine. Bus drops you at Gyeongju Express Bus Terminal; taxi to guesthouse (~₩8,000–12,000).
  • Gyeongju has no subway: Use local city buses (Naver Maps handles Korean bus routing well) or taxis, which are inexpensive.
  • T-money card: Get one at Incheon on arrival. Top up ₩50,000. Works on all subways and buses.
Day 2 — Gyeongju → Busan (Jul 4, Fri)
~130km (Intercity Bus) ~2h transit
TimeDurationActivity
09:00–09:40~40 minWonjo Kongguk (원조 콩국수). Walk 5 min from your hanok guesthouse. Order warm soybean soup (kongguk) with sticky rice balls to share, plus a pajeon (scallion pancake). Fast, comforting fuel before the temples.
09:40–10:30~50 minBus to Bulguksa. Catch Bus No. 10 or 11 east toward the mountains (~30–40 min).
10:30–11:00~30 minShuttle to Seokguram Grotto. At the Bulguksa bus stop, do NOT enter the temple yet. Immediately line up for Bus No. 12 (shuttle runs once per hour, usually at the bottom of the hour).
11:00–12:00~1hSeokguram Grotto (석굴암). Beautiful shaded 15-min walk through forest to the grotto. Small glass-enclosed stone chamber with an 8th-century Buddha statue — no photos allowed. Lines form fast in July; you get about 10 minutes inside. Walk back to parking lot, catch Bus No. 12 down (usually leaves top at :15 past the hour).
12:30–14:00~1.5hBulguksa Temple (불국사). Now explore at your own pace. Walk up to see the iconic double-staircase stone bridges (Cheongungyo and Baegungyo) — UNESCO-listed, most famous photo spot. Main courtyard (Daeungjeon): two massive contrasting pagodas — Dabotap (ornate, look for the single remaining stone lion at the base; Japanese stole three during occupation) and Seokgatap (minimalist, austere). Behind the main hall in Geungnakjeon area: look for the famous wooden pig carving hidden under the eaves, then rub the golden pig statue in the courtyard for good luck!
14:00–14:40~40 minBus back to city. Catch Bus 10 or 11 into central Gyeongju.
14:40–15:30~50 minPick up luggage at your hanok guesthouse.
15:30–16:00~30 minTaxi to Gyeongju Intercity Bus Terminal (~₩8,000–12,000).
16:00–17:45~1h45mIntercity Bus: Gyeongju → Busan. Buy ticket for '부산종합버스터미널' at terminal. ₩10,000–15,000.
17:45–18:00~15 minTaxi or subway from Dong-Busan Terminal to Toyoko Inn Jungang Station. Check in, drop bags.
18:00–19:30~1.5hJagalchi Market (자갈치시장) — Korea's largest seafood market. Walk the multi-level building, watch live fish and shellfish being sold, see the auction floor. Have your selection cooked to order at restaurants on upper floors.
19:30–20:30~1hBIFF Square (비프광장) — Historic square with street food, movie posters from Busan International Film Festival. Try the ssiat hotteok (sweet seed-filled pancakes).
20:30–21:00~30 minGukje Market (국제시장). Directly across from BIFF Square, right behind the street food stalls. Sprawling maze-like traditional market — vintage clothing, electronics, Korean homewares. Fantastic for getting lost and souvenir shopping.
21:00–21:30~30 minBusan Tower / Yongdusan Park. Look up from Nampo-dong — Busan Tower glows on the hill above. Take the giant covered outdoor escalator up from the main shopping street to Yongdusan Park (~8:00–8:30 PM). Beautiful quiet park with sweeping night views of the harbor. Pay to go up the tower or enjoy free views from the park base.
AccommodationToyoko Inn Busan Jungang Station (東京堂인釜山中央駅). Right on Line 1 subway station. Clean, reliable Japanese chain — no-frills but functional and well-located for transit.
Trip.com Booking
FoodBreakfast: Wonjo Kongguk (원조 콩국수) — warm soybean soup with sticky rice balls + pajeon scallion pancake. Opens 9 AM, walk from guesthouse.
Lunch: Quick lunch in Gyeongju after Bulguksa (~1:00 PM).
Dinner: Seafood at Jagalchi Market (cooked to order) + ssiat hotteok at BIFF Square.

Practical Tips

  • Gyeongju → Busan transit: Taxi from guesthouse to Gyeongju Express Bus Terminal (경주터미널) ~₩8,000–12,000 (15 min). Buy ticket to 'Busan' (부산) — look for '부산종합버스터미널'. Buses every 30–60 min, ~1h45m–2h journey, ₩10,000–15,000. Bus drops you at Dong-Busan Terminal (동부산터미널) — take subway Line 1 westbound toward Jagalchi (3 stops, ~8 min) to Toyoko Inn Jungang Station area.
  • Temple bus strategy: Bus 10 or 11 from central Gyeongju to Bulguksa stop. From there, Bus No. 12 shuttle goes up to Seokguram (runs hourly). Do Seokguram FIRST, then Bulguksa — biggest mistake is doing Bulguksa first and missing the shuttle.
  • Busan transit: Excellent subway. Jungang Station (Line 1) is directly at your hotel — Jagalchi Market is 2 stops, BIFF Square/Gukje Market area is nearby.
  • Yongdusan escalator: Giant covered outdoor escalator on main Nampo-dong shopping street takes you straight up to Yongdusan Park — no hiking needed.
Day 3 — Busan → Seoul (Jul 5, Sat)
~380km (Intercity Bus) ~4h transit (~7pm–11pm)
TimeDurationActivity
07:30–09:00~1.5hBreakfast at Toyoko Inn. Japanese-style buffet included in room rate — steamed rice, miso soup (often with clams), pickled vegetables, tamagoyaki (rolled omelette). Decent for a budget hotel: reliable and filling, not gourmet. Leave luggage at front desk or in room.
09:30–11:00~1.5hHaedong Yonggungsa Temple (해동용궁사) — One of the few temples in Korea built right on the coastline. The main hall sits on a rocky outcrop with waves crashing below — stunning views and peaceful atmosphere.

Take Bus 132 from Jungang Station area (~30 min). Arrive by 9:30 to beat the crowds.
11:30–12:30~1hLunch: Haeundae Beach area — seafood restaurants along the coast. Try fresh sashimi or grilled fish.
12:30–15:30~3hHaeundae Beach Walk (해운대해수욕장). Flat, easy stroll along Busan's most famous beach. Crystal clear water in July. Walk the full length of the beach promenade — no hiking, no stairs, no cable car or train needed. Refreshing dip if you dare (it's cold!).

Continue to Mipo → Cheongsapo Coastal Boardwalk (미포→청사포 해안 산책로) — gorgeous, easy 2–3 km cliffside path with ocean views the whole way. Free, no booking needed.

Cheongsapo (청사포). Explore the village — twin lighthouses (Cheongsapo Light) and ocean-view cafes. Relaxed, artsy vibe.
15:30–16:30~1hReturn to Toyoko Inn. Pick up luggage from front desk.
16:30–18:00~1.5hPuffer Fish Dinner (복어 전문점). Head to Nampo-dong/Jagalchi area — several dedicated puffer fish restaurants nearby. Puffer fish (bokeo) is typically served as sashimi (회), grilled (구이), or in hot pot (전골). Prices: ₩30,000–80,000 per person depending on restaurant and preparation. Search "복어전문점" on Naver Maps near Jagalchi Market.
18:30–19:00~30 minTransit to Busan Express Bus Terminal (부산종합버스터미널). Subway Line 1 from Jungang Station to Busan Station (2 stops), terminal is connected.
19:00–23:00~4hIntercity Bus: Busan → Seoul Nambu Terminal. Your 7 PM ticket. Arrive ~11:00 PM. ₩25,000–35,000 (already purchased).
23:00–23:45~45 minTaxi from Seoul Nambu Terminal to Grand Intercontinental COEX (~₩25,000–35,000). Or subway Line 3 express to Yeoksam (Line 2) — about 30 min.
23:45—Check into hotel. Drop bags, rest before ICML.
AccommodationGrand Intercontinental Seoul COEX (그랜드 인터컨티넨탈 서울 코엑스). Right at the conference venue. Ultra-convenient for ICML days.
Trip.com Booking
FoodBreakfast: Toyoko Inn Japanese-style buffet (included in room rate).
Lunch: Haeundae Beach area — seafood restaurants along the coast.
Dinner: Puffer fish (복어) at a specialty restaurant near Jagalchi Market/Nampo-dong.

Practical Tips

  • Luggage: Leave at Toyoko Inn front desk or in room. No need for coin lockers — you're returning to the hotel before your evening bus.
  • Bus to Seoul (7 PM): You already have this ticket. Head to Busan Express Bus Terminal by 6:30 PM for boarding.
  • Haeundae Beach walk: Flat, easy promenade — no cable car or train needed. Allow 2–3 hours for the full beach + Mipo→Cheongsapo boardwalk (~5 km total).
  • Puffer fish (복어): Search "복어전문점" on Naver Maps near Jagalchi Market. Look for restaurants with 4.0+ stars and Korean reviews. Typical dishes: sashimi (회), grilled (구이), hot pot (전골). ₩30,000–80,000 per person.
